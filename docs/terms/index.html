WHOOP Lens is an independent integration. It is not affiliated with WHOOP. WHOOP does not make, operate, or endorse it.

You connect your own WHOOP account and sign in with your own Supabase user. WHOOP Lens reads data you permit so ChatGPT and Codex can answer your questions.

Read-only WHOOP access

WHOOP access is read-only. The connection can read your profile, body measurements, recovery, sleep, workouts, and cycles. It does not request write access, and it does not change, add, or delete anything in your WHOOP account.

Revoking access

You can revoke this integration in your WHOOP account settings. Revoking it stops future WHOOP reads through WHOOP Lens.

Answers already received

ChatGPT may keep answers it already received. Revoking WHOOP access does not delete those answers. They remain under your ChatGPT settings. The same is true of answers Codex already received.

How data is handled is described in the privacy statement. For help, use the support page.
