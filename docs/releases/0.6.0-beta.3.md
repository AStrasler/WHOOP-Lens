# WHOOP Lens Android 0.6.0-beta.3 candidate

Android versionCode: 10

- Fixes the Home recovery card stretching and losing its title: source badges now use compact highlight insets instead of the pill corner radius as a minimum-width inset.
- Replaces Home's repeated partial-history warnings with one compact source-status summary and a direct link to full Sources & Freshness details. Measurement attribution and source limitations remain available.
- Displays WHOOP skin temperatures and Health Connect body temperatures in Fahrenheit (°F), including measurement history, charts, statistics and personal baselines. Original provider/cache measurements stay unchanged. Body and skin temperature remain separately attributed.

Includes regression coverage for badge/title sizing at normal/enlarged fonts and Fahrenheit conversion without changing cached values. Fresh CI determines verification status. Physical Samsung/One UI acceptance remains required under PR #112.

This candidate follows the published signed beta.2 (versionCode 9). Never replace that release or reuse its version code. Signing/publication uses the protected Android release workflow. Install a future signed update over the existing app; do not uninstall or clear data.
