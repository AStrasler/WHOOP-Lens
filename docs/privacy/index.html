WHOOP Lens

Privacy statement

Last updated: October 20, 2026

WHOOP Lens is a private, read-only MCP that lets a person connect their own WHOOP account so ChatGPT and Codex can read that person's data with their permission. The project is operated by Aaron Strasler. It is not affiliated with WHOOP.

WHOOP Lens runs on Cloudflare. The MCP server is https://whooplens.tech/mcp and its authorization server is https://auth.whooplens.tech. Sign-in is a Cloudflare Access email one-time PIN. Access also requires an active WHOOP Lens membership granted by the owner; accounts are never created or linked automatically by email.

Data accessed and purpose

With your authorization, the plugin reads WHOOP recovery, sleep, physiological cycles and strain, workouts, profile information, and body measurements. The WHOOP connection requests read scopes only (read:profile, read:body_measurement, read:recovery, read:sleep, read:workout, and read:cycles), plus offline so access can be refreshed.

If Samsung Health records are stored for your account in the WHOOP Lens Cloudflare D1 database, the plugin can also read those stored records, such as sleep, heart rate, blood oxygen, steps, activity, exercise, body composition, skin temperature, energy score, blood pressure, and profile. Samsung Health records are kept separate from WHOOP data, are read only for the signed-in account, and are not sold or used for advertising.

This information is used to answer your questions and produce the summaries you request in ChatGPT or Codex.

Processing and sharing

The Cloudflare Workers handle sign-in, consent, the WHOOP authorization callback, WHOOP token refresh, and WHOOP API requests. WHOOP access and refresh tokens are not placed in the ChatGPT or Codex authorization grant.

Data returned to ChatGPT or Codex is processed by OpenAI under your OpenAI account settings and the OpenAI privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/.

WHOOP data is not sold and is not used for advertising. This privacy statement is published at https://astrasler.github.io/WHOOP-Lens/privacy/. WHOOP records, credentials, and access tokens are not published in the git repository.

Credentials, retention, and revocation

WHOOP access and refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, bound to your account, and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database so the plugin can read WHOOP and refresh authorization. Pending WHOOP authorization requests are stored only as a hash and expire after 10 minutes.

Disconnecting WHOOP through WHOOP Lens deletes your stored WHOOP connection and pending authorization requests. Disconnecting through WHOOP Lens requires an active membership. If your membership has ended, revoking in WHOOP stops future access but does not delete the stored connection; request that deletion through the support page. You can also revoke the plugin in your WHOOP account settings. Revocation stops future authorized WHOOP access. It does not automatically delete information already included in ChatGPT or Codex conversations. Conversations remain subject to your OpenAI settings and controls.

WHOOP Lens has a limited number of connection seats, set by the owner and never more than 10, the owner's included. A person who does not already have a WHOOP connection cannot complete a new one when all seats are in use. Seats are granted only by the owner after a seat request; there is no self-registration. When seat requests are enabled, a request is emailed to whoop-lens@outlook.com and checked by Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse. Hashed request metadata is used for duplicate and abuse protection; form contents are not stored in the request database. The seat request form asks you not to include health information, passwords, or tokens, and it does not include WHOOP access tokens or health data from your account.

Private Cloudflare MCP and optional processing

The private MCP verifies OAuth identity and active membership. Health Connect phone delivery requires separate consent and a per-member token stored as a hash. Withdrawal deletes that member's Health Connect records and revokes ingest tokens. In-progress uploads cannot write after withdrawal.

On-demand summaries send minimized numerical facts and precomputed statements to Cloudflare Workers AI, excluding free-text notes, credentials and account identifiers. WHOOP Lens stores daily usage counters and optional account-scoped weekly digests containing derived health facts and charts, but not model prompts. Disconnecting WHOOP through WHOOP Lens invalidates that account's digest cache. Experimental stress estimates are WHOOP Lens calculations, not WHOOP Stress Monitor measurements or clinically validated results.

Optional diagnostics and issue reports (owner-visible)

These features are off unless the server owner enables them. They are the one place where the server owner sees information about other members.

When diagnostic capture is enabled, WHOOP Lens records sanitized failure codes for every signed-in member, whether or not they submit a report: an error code, the affected area, a count and a time window, linked to the account. No health values, message text or tokens are stored. The server owner can see server-wide 24-hour counts, and on a small server may be able to tell which member a count came from. Codes are deleted after 30 days.

When issue reporting is enabled, a member can send a report after previewing and approving the cleaned text. Cleaning is best-effort: it removes numbers, links, contact details and recognizable credentials, but a secret written in another form can be missed, and every other word, including any health or personal details the member writes, is stored and shown to the server owner as written. Leave health details, personal details and passwords out of a report. A report also attaches up to 10 of that member's recent diagnostic codes. The owner sees reports under a stable pseudonymous member reference, not an account id, and can change only a report's status.

Unapproved previews expire after 10 minutes. Reports are kept until the owner resolves them, then deleted 180 days after their last update.

Optional flare journal (separate opt-in)

When enabled, the private flare journal requires explicit consent to store user-provided condition names, optional medication context, dated flare/non-flare/unknown labels, severity, symptoms, confounders and notes in account-scoped Cloudflare D1 tables. A separate explicit consent permits on-demand descriptive comparison with that member's WHOOP measurements. Membership and existing health consent grant neither purpose. No context is inferred from chat memory.

Entries remain until deleted or journal consent is withdrawn. Analysis can be paused independently. Withdrawal deletes the account's context and journal and revokes both purposes; a minimal consent state remains to enforce withdrawal. Journal data is not sent to Workers AI, used for training, shared across members or included in developer analytics. Analyses are not persisted or cached and do not produce validated predictions or notifications. Requested results appear in the user's chat under the host's retention rules. Deletion does not erase existing chat messages or separate WHOOP/Health Connect records.
