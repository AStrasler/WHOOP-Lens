# Native glass refinements

The HRV/RHR personal-baseline cards, liquid buttons and icon-led drawer are integrated with main's Overview cards, recorded chart points, freshness labels and Home controls. Overview compares prior records; HRV/RHR explicitly compare a personal baseline. Neither arrow implies health improvement.

Recovery uses a separate details button so nested HRV/RHR controls stay independently reachable. Stat cards expose one button role and one complete accessible name; decorative children are hidden from accessibility. Font scaling above 130% stacks the cards. Touch targets are at least 48dp. Lift effects are suppressed for remove-animations, reduced transparency and battery saver.

## Automated and owner verification

Android CI runs unit tests, lint, debug build and instrumentation compilation. The API 34/36 emulator job produces 18 synthetic component screenshots per API: 360/412/480dp widths, 100%/200% fonts, and three appearances. It checks text fit, independent taps, touch targets, accessible roles, contrast and remove-animations. CI also sets the emulator to 1440×3120 pixels at logical density 560 and exercises actual Home HRV/recovery navigation and drawer-to-settings at both font scales. The resolution matches [Samsung's published S26 Ultra specification](https://www.samsung.com/uk/business/smartphones/galaxy-s/galaxy-s26-ultra-cobalt-violet-256gb-sm-s948bzvdeub/); logical density is a test assumption, not a verified owner display-zoom setting. These are representative density-independent phone widths and stock Android renders, not One UI behavior or physical-device approval.
