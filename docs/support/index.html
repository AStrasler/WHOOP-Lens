Support

The private MCP server is https://whooplens.tech/mcp. Authorization server: https://auth.whooplens.tech. Scope: mcp:read. Sign-in is a Cloudflare Access email one-time PIN.

To ask for help or report a problem with WhoopLens, open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/AStrasler/WHOOP-Lens/issues

Say what you were trying to do and what happened. Do not include passwords, WHOOP tokens, or exported health data in the issue.

Private requests and data deletion

GitHub issues are public. For anything private, including deleting your data, email whoop-lens@outlook.com from the address you sign in with. Do not include passwords, WHOOP tokens or health data.

With an active membership you can delete data yourself in WhoopLens: disconnecting WHOOP deletes the stored WHOOP connection, pending authorization requests and any cached weekly AI digest; withdrawing Health Connect consent deletes your Health Connect records and revokes upload tokens; disconnecting Notion deletes the stored Notion credentials and settings (your Notion pages are not deleted); withdrawing journal consent deletes your context and journal.

If your membership has ended, these in-app options are no longer available. Email the address above to ask for your stored WHOOP connection and other stored data to be deleted. The owner handles these requests by hand: deletion is not automatic and there is no fixed response time. Information already in ChatGPT or Codex conversations is controlled by your OpenAI settings, not by WhoopLens.
